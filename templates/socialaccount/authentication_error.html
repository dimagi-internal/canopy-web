This page is reached far more often than "third-party login failure" implies. allauth's `verify_and_unstash_state` POPS the state from the session and Google's `code` is single-use, so ANY replay of a callback URL lands here by construction: a restored tab, a back-button press, a reload, an autocompleted URL from history.
