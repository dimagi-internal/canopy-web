# Pages: scoped public viewers and root (personal, global, pre-tenant) pages

The anonymous token-scoped viewers, and the root pages: the public site, getting started, pairing, connections, act-as, home, supervisor, IDE, personal settings, people and invites. Part of the [pages](pages.md) topic; the index there lists every part. What each page shows is stated by its component: find it by path in `frontend/src/router.tsx` and by purpose in `frontend/src/guide/surfaces.ts`.

## Scoped public viewers (anonymous with a token)

- **Which paths are anonymous** is `_SCOPED_VIEWER` in `apps/common/middleware.py`, mirrored by `SCOPED_VIEWER_RE` in `frontend/src/lib/scopedLinks.ts`: `/w/<ws>/{walkthrough,review,share,storyboard,narrative,ddd-release,app}/…`. The trailing slash is load-bearing: the plural list pages (`/walkthroughs`, `/storyboards`, `/apps`) stay behind login. Each viewer passes `ws` to its self-gating API so a row from another workspace is a 404.
- **Each viewer self-gates on its token** (`?t=` for walkthrough, storyboard, review and ddd-release; `b=<storyboard>` for a narrative; the app being public for `/app/`). The walkthrough's bytes are a bare Django view with Range support and a sandbox CSP (`apps/walkthroughs/streaming.py`). `/share/:token` mounts outside `AppLayout` with its own `ShareRouteErrorBoundary`.
- **`/review/:id` is not a narrative surface:** a narrative review is read and decided on its storyboard, so the address forwards there with a bare 302 before the SPA. The token rules (the board's share token goes only to someone holding the review's) are in `apps/reviews/views.py`; a findings review (no narrative) still renders in the SPA. The bridge to the DDD loop is `apps/storyboards/review_gate.py`.
- **Storyboard feedback rounds and decision rows:** the rules (who opens, closes and sees notes; closing makes one task and one turn) are `apps/storyboards/rounds.py` and `apps/storyboards/decisions.py`. Acts are storage grouping only; readers see narratives, never "acts" (`apps/storyboards/models.py::Act`).
- **The narrative reviewer surface shows the story only** (no gates, features, provenance or findings); the operator console is `/ddd/:narrative`, behind a link.

## Root (personal, global, pre-tenant)

Root pages carry a `flat` reason in `frontend/src/guide/surfaces.ts` (typically: it is about you or the fleet, not a workspace, or no workspace exists yet).

- **`/` is the public product site for everyone** (static Astro pages from `site/`, served by `config/public_site.py`; only paths that exist in the build are the site's, and WhiteNoise leaves `/` to Django, `config/static_middleware.py`). `/app` is the way into the app. The shared Dimagi stylesheet `site/public/site/styles.css` is vendored unchanged: do not edit it; the theme is `site/public/site/canopy-theme.css` (colour only). The Request access form posts to the anonymous `POST /api/beta-requests` (`apps/beta_requests`), which stores and emails and grants nothing.
- **`/download/runner/<platform>`** is a server view, not the SPA (`apps/harness/views.py::download_runner`); `/get-started#runner` offers it.
- **`/pair/:code`** approves a computer asking to become your runner. It is root because a pairing has no workspace until you pick one; approving needs a passkey step-up (`apps/harness/api_runner_pairings.py`).
- **`/listen`** records a meeting on a phone for its owner's laptop to transcribe, end-to-end encrypted ([`listen-relay.md`](listen-relay.md)). It is root because it is about your phone and your laptop, and outside the app shell because it is a phone screen. Its shell is served to anyone (`apps/common/middleware.py::_PUBLIC_PAGES`) so `frontend/src/listen/pairLink.ts::captureListenPairing` can take a QR link's `#pair=` secret out of the address before the app sends a signed-out phone to sign in; the page itself needs a session.
- **Connections:** `/w/:workspace/connections/:id/connected` is where the OAuth callback lands, and `…/share` is the choice of who may use it. Their behaviour is the header comments of `pages/connections/ConnectedPage.tsx` and `ShareConnectionPage.tsx`. Invariants: the page strips `just_connected` once read so a reload or shared link never prompts; `return_to` must be a same-site path (`safeReturnTo`); the unlocked key lives in a ref, never state; an unfinished first connect is never listed.
- **`/act-as/:requestId` and `/confirm/page-permission/:grantId`** are about you, not a workspace, and only the person they are for can open them. Approving runs a passkey assertion made for that request, and approve and decline refuse every token (`apps/harness/api_act_as.py`).
- **Passkeys do not work inside the Canopy Runner app**, so every passkey moment there opens in the browser and the app's page follows the decision made there (`frontend/src/auth/passkeyHere.ts`; `apps/passkeys/stepup.py` refuses step-up on a token-minted session).
- **`/home`** is one user-level read across every workspace (`GET /api/me/attention/`, `apps/attention/`, plus `GET /api/me/alerts/`; [`alerts.md`](alerts.md)). Hide only takes a thing off your own Home: the task stays open and nobody is told, until something new happens on it. `/app` lands here.
- **`/supervisor`** is root because the fleet spans workspaces; pushes are only about its Feed (`apps/canopy_sessions/feed.py`). It is loaded by the phone PWA, desktop browsers and the runner menubar's web view.
- **`/ide`** is the desktop session IDE (`frontend/src/pages/ide/`), root because it spans workspaces; phones use `/supervisor`. Its filters are objective on/off toggles kept in `localStorage`; nothing is ranked or labelled "stuck". The close review has the agent read the visible sessions through a page action answered by this tab as you and propose with `propose_session_closes`; only your click closes anything (`pages/ide/useCloseReview.ts`).
- **`/settings/<section>`** is everything about you, the same in every workspace: sections are `SETTINGS_SECTIONS` in `frontend/src/pages/settingsSections.ts`, no section has tabs, and every old `/settings#anchor` or `?device_key=` / `?github=` link forwards to the section holding it, keeping query and hash. Memory keeps `/people/me` and is in the rail only for people it is on for.
- **`/people/:personId/page`** is a person's memory page, read-only, for an agent they let see it; anyone not let in sees nothing, and every look is logged (`apps/contacts/viewers.py`).
- **`/hcp-clients`** is for superusers; **`/beta-requests`** (+ `/:requestId`) is for the requests' reviewer or a superuser (`apps/beta_requests`, `@gate("beta-reviewer")`).
- **`/new-workspace`** (`FirstRunPage`): a workspace that holds the coach (`COACHING_AGENT_SLUG`) offers an unticked "have the coach coach me" box. On a request approved at once it grants a session review ([`tenancy-session-access-and-retention.md`](tenancy-session-access-and-retention.md) § The chat-session ACL); on a pending one it grants nothing and points to the switch in account settings.
- **`/invite/:token`** is outside `/w/` because an invitee has no membership yet. It previews before login (masked email, workspace, role: `GET /api/workspaces/invites/{token}/preview`); accepting needs a login.
- **Legacy flat redirects** to the active workspace are the `NOT_PAGES` entries in `frontend/src/guide/coverage.ts`. An unmatched path renders `NotFound`, which reloads once for a stale bundle before showing a real 404 (`reloadOnceForStaleBundle`).
