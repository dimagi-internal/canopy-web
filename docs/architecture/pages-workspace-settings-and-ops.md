# Pages: workspace settings, sessions, health and the rest

The tenant-scoped pages for workspace settings, sessions and chat, huddles, threads, schedules, activity, runner invitations, members, sign-offs, health, analytics and network, shareouts, walkthroughs, storyboards and DDD. Part of the [pages](pages.md) topic; the index there lists every part. What each page shows is stated by its component: find it by path in `frontend/src/router.tsx` and by purpose in `frontend/src/guide/surfaces.ts`.

## Workspace settings (`/w/:ws/settings/<section>`)

- **One page, one section per URL** (`pages/WorkspaceSettingsPage.tsx`; index → `members`). Each section fetches its own data, and one-way actions belong on a section opened on purpose. Gates per section follow `apps/workspaces/permissions.py` ([`roles.md`](roles.md), [`access.md`](access.md)). `/w/:ws/{members,inbound,slack,host-sites}` redirect to their section, keeping query and hash. Root `/settings` is your account, not this.
- **Members:** who may grant which role is `apps/workspaces/permissions.py`. The member-tree matrix is `GET /api/workspaces/{slug}/member-tree` (`apps/workspaces/api.py::member_tree` states what inherits: owners and admins of an ancestor, not editors or viewers). A new invite's link is emailed only when email is enabled (`CANOPY_EMAIL_ENABLED`, `apps/common/email.py`); otherwise the page says so and leaves the link to copy.
- **Slack:** one Slack installation can serve several workspaces; a message's tenant is decided by its thread's session or the agent it names (`apps/slack/models.py::SlackInstallation`). Declaring the app an agent is one-way and owner-only (`apps/slack/api.py::declare_agent`).
- **Secrets** (`pages/WorkspaceSecretsPage.tsx`) is the one screen that states the credential model whole: its header comment is the model. Task settings: the key prefix changes only while no task carries it (`apps/agents/api_tasks.py`).
- **Topology** (`pages/topology/`) is the fleet map and where runners are configured on the web: its side panel mounts the supervisor's `RunnerDetail` and the agent's `AgentRouting`, and it is drawn from the same two endpoints as the table views so the picture cannot disagree with them. `topology/runners` and `topology/agents` read `apps/harness/topology.py` and `apps/agents/topology.py`; both are admin and above (`logs.read`) on every descendant, and routes are edited on the agent. In `topology/agents`, a grant names who could then steer the receiver through the sender, because agent admin is transitive; it is the receiver's owner or a workspace owner who grants (`PUT /api/agents/{slug}/admins/{user_id}`). Routing semantics: [`runners.md`](runners.md).

## Sessions and chat

- **`/sessions`** is the workspace's sessions plus those of the workspaces below it (`ChatSessionsPanel` on `/api/w/{ws}/canopy-sessions/`); "Started by me" keeps only sessions you started or found on your own box (`SessionOut.started_by_you`, `apps/canopy_sessions/access.py::is_own_session`).
- **`/sessions/:id`** is live multiplayer chat (`ChatPanel` + `useSessionSocket` over `ws/canopy-sessions/{id}/`); a send enqueues a session `Turn` that a session-capable runner drives.
- **A private (sensitive) conversation opens behind an interstitial** (`components/chat/PrivateConversationGate.tsx`, also /ide's pane and the embed). Anyone else who can see it may only ask its starter to add them; the request shows in the conversation (`JoinRequests`), never on Home. A private conversation is never cached or painted early (`pages/chat/sessionCache.ts`).
- **"Delete this session's content"** shows only when `can_delete_content`; a deleted session stays openable, empty, with its composer refused ([`tenancy-session-access-and-retention.md`](tenancy-session-access-and-retention.md) § Deleting your own session's content). **"Ask <coach> to review this"** is the session's own person's (`SessionReview.tsx`, [`access-sessions-and-people.md`](access-sessions-and-people.md) § Asking a coach to review one session).

## Gated pages

- **`/runner-invitations/:invitationId`:** only the invitation's creator can open it (404 otherwise) or approve it, and a token cannot; the passkey step-up is the API client's (`apps/harness/api_runner_invitations.py`, [`runner-credentials.md`](runner-credentials.md) § How a runner signs in).
- **`/sign-offs/:signOffId`:** only the person it is for, or the agent's owner and admins, can open it (404 otherwise); a token cannot approve (`apps/host_grants/sign_offs_api.py`).
- **`/health`, `/analytics`, `/network` are admin and above (`logs.read`), per descendant workspace** (`apps/agents/api_workspace_health.py`). Health renders and never decides: it reuses the agent's own setup checklist, and its runner table is the one shared with Topology → Runners (`components/runners/RunnersTable.tsx`). Its figures come from `apps/agents/health.py`, `apps/events/analytics.py` and [`analytics.md`](analytics.md). A network thread opens only if the viewer may open that chat.
- **`/threads/:id`:** a message is refused at enqueue when it falls outside its thread's limits (`apps/threads/guard.py`, which states them).
- **`/huddles`** is derived, nothing stored (`apps/huddles`); a round turn's content is gated by `turn_access`.
- **`/marketplace.json` and `/plugins/…`** answer **410** naming the one personal catalog, `/plugins/marketplace.json` ([`plugin-marketplace.md`](plugin-marketplace.md)).
